Energy Usage

  • On average a data center can consume up to 2 mega watt hours of electricity per day, which is equivalent to a small town. In 2023 they consumed 4% of the US electricity, with some expecting it to rise up to 12% in 2028. Some data centers can consume as much energy as 100,000 households and there are some data centers in construction that are estimated to possibly use 20 times that amount.

  • U.S. data centers consumed 183 terawatt-hours (TWh) of electricity in 2024. Data centers accounted for more than 4% of total US electricity consumption, based on 2,132 US data centers operating between September 2023 and August 2024. (Cornell University)

  • Source: IEA
