Stakeholders

  • Group 1

    A bunch of independent people and families who live in the area of the data center. They are all worried about the possible impacts of the new data center, because they have heard that they use massive amounts of energy and water to run. Their interests in keeping their environment clean and sustainable should be considered. Data centers do also improve their internet use sometimes.

  • Group 2

    The company who wants to build the data center. They need it to provide better internet and services to their customers. Since the customer base is growing they need a data center to be able to handle this growing base, if not it could be a problem for their profits and they may not be able to handle an overflow. Their interests in keeping their customers happy and raising profits should be considered.

  • Group 3

    The people who benefit/use the services provided by datacenters. Since datacenters power pretty much of modern internet service, pretty much everybody is a part of this. Users will benefit from faster connection, and more reliable internet services. The interests for this group is that everybody wants good internet and this group is far larger than any other group and more impactful in the world. People do also want a clean environment and some people may care more about internet use over environment and vice versa
