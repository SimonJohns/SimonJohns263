Building in Already Scarce Regions

  • Research shows that one fifth of data centers draw water from already moderately or highly water stressed regions, while almost half are powered or support by power plants drawing from water stressed regions. These facts raise concerns about resource competition with local communities. (Siddik, M. A. B., Shehabi, A., & Marston, L. 2021).

  • This creates ethical issues around environmental justice, especially in areas prone to drought. While these projects may bring economic benefits, they often provide relatively few long term jobs compared to their environmental costs. As shown below, many of the major data center hotspots overlap with regions scarce in water. Las Vegas, Phoenix, and Salt Lake City hotspots are all built in the most water scarce regions and are all projected to increase their capacity, most sepecially Las Vegas.
